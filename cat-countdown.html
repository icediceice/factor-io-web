    Questions, answered.

    What is Cat Countdown?

    A countdown app where every date gets a cat. Add a birthday, an exam, a trip or a due date, and one of nine cats appears in an illustrated scene made for that kind of day. Around the art sits a cosy organiser: tasks, decorated notes, alarms and home screen widgets.

    How much does it cost?

    You pay once on Google Play and it is yours, with no ads and no fees to keep using it. All nine cats and all 1,000+ scenes are included. The one extra is optional Cloud Backup, a yearly subscription you can skip.

    Is Cat Countdown on iPhone?

    The iPhone and iPad version is waiting for App Store review and is coming soon. The Android version is available on Google Play now.

    Where are my countdowns stored?

    On your phone. Factor IO has no user accounts and never receives the content you create. If you turn on Cloud Backup, the copy goes to your own Google Drive or iCloud.

    Can I move my countdowns to a new phone?

    Yes. Cloud Backup copies your data to your own cloud storage, and you restore it on the new phone. Restoring a backup you already have is always free. It is backup and restore, not live sync between devices.

    Do the alarms really ring?

    Yes. Set a reminder to ring as an alarm, and your cat rings on screen until you snooze or dismiss it.

    What happens when the day arrives?

    The countdown is not deleted. It moves to History, so you can look back on every day you counted down to.

    Can I keep my tickets and bookings in it?

    Yes. Attach photos to a note, or add files and links to a countdown, and it works as a clipboard for the day: the concert ticket, the hotel reservation, the boarding pass.

    Does the background really follow the weather?

    If you turn it on, the app asks Open-Meteo for your local weather and draws it behind your countdowns. You can also pick a seasonal background, a solid colour, or a random dynamic one such as starlight, fireflies or glitter.

    Is there a home screen widget?

    Yes, in small, medium or large. It shows what is coming next and rolls the number over at midnight without you opening the app.

    Can I rename the cats?

    Yes. Keep their names or rename any of them.

    Which languages does it support?

    English, German, French, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese and Chinese.
