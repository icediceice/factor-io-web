Buyer Assurance 01 / PDPA s.28 & s.29

          
Eliminates Cross-Border Personal Data Transfer

          

            Calling an overseas LLM API with a Thai customer's name, 13-digit National ID, or transfer slip is a cross-border personal data transfer under PDPA Sections 28 and 29. Running fine-tuned models inside your Thailand data center avoids cross-border transfer mechanisms entirely, while scrubbing PII in volatile RAM before context ingestion.
          

        

      

      

        

          
Buyer Assurance 02 / Fail-Closed Control

          
Never a False Green When Upstream Breaks

          

            The most dangerous failure in enterprise automation is a dashboard that stays green when an upstream scanner or ERP link goes silent. LightOps AI evaluates lane completeness as an early return: if an OCR check or SAP lookup is unavailable, the transaction halts as UNKNOWN and can never be outvoted by a model confidence score.
          

        

      

      

        

          
Buyer Assurance 03 / Vector Authority

          
Right-to-Erasure & Document Lifecycle Lineage

          

            Most RAG builds cannot answer how to honour a PDPA deletion request or retire a superseded pricing policy once embedded. Every vector chunk in LightOps AI is keyed to its authoritative source ID, effective date window, and role ACL—allowing deterministic purge and instant retirement of superseded documents.
