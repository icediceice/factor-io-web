Privacy Statement

Factor I O Co., Ltd. · Effective 20 August 2026
This statement applies to Cat Countdown on Android and iPhone, and to every Factor IO application that does not ship its own privacy policy. (Blink maintains its own privacy policy.)
01 / The short version
  • No advertising. Our apps show no ads, ever.
  • Nothing collected. We do not gather, store, or receive any of your data.
  • Data leaves your device only if you choose to back it up — and then it goes only to your own cloud account, never to us.
02 / No advertising

Our apps carry no ads and no tracking

Cat Countdown contains no advertisements, no advertising networks or SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We do not earn money from your attention, so we have no reason to watch you.

03 / No data collection

We do not collect your data

We collect, store, and transmit no personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, and no telemetry. Everything you create in the app — your countdowns, dates, and settings — stays on your device.

04 / The only exception: backups you choose

Data leaves your device only when you ask it to

There is exactly one way data can leave your device, and it is entirely your choice: the optional backup feature. When you turn it on, your data is uploaded to your own cloud account:

  • Android — backup to your Google Drive.
  • iPhone — backup to your iCloud.

The backup lives inside your personal account with that provider and is governed by their privacy policy. Factor IO never receives, sees, or stores it. If you never turn backup on, no data ever leaves your device.

05 / Third-party services

Only the ones you choose

The only third parties ever involved are the cloud providers you opt into by enabling backup. We have no arrangement with them that gives us access to your data. Their policies: Google · Apple.

06 / Children’s privacy

Safe for everyone

Because our apps collect no data from anyone, they collect no data from children either. Nothing is ever transmitted to us by any user of any age.

07 / Changes to this statement

We will tell you

If this statement changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. If a change ever affects how an app handles your data, we will surface it in the app itself.
