How this works

Compares three lanes — owned local hardware, a cloud model API, rented GPU nodes — over your demand, horizon and routing policy. Prices come from cited public feeds with observed-at timestamps; arithmetic is decimal-exact; every number carries provenance. Modelled throughput verdicts say unknown unless a cited benchmark matches your configuration on every dimension. Demand is tokenizer-independent by convention: entries are reference tokens for your declared tokenizer. No cross-model conversion is applied in v0.1 — quoting a model whose billing units differ is labelled estimated, never silently 1:1.
