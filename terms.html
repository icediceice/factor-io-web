  • Your storage provider’s terms apply. The backup uses space in your Google or Apple account and is subject to that account’s quota, availability and terms. If your account is full, suspended or deleted, or you revoke the app’s access to it, backups can fail or be lost, and that is outside our control.

Keep your device and your storage account in good order, and do not rely on Cat Countdown as the only record of anything you cannot afford to lose.
